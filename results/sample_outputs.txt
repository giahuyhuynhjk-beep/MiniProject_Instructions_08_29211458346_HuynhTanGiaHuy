[3.3] Analytical Cold-Start Explanation:
  * Why Collaborative Filtering Fails: User-Based CF relies on existing user-item interaction matrices.
    A brand-new article has zero ratings from any user, creating an empty rating vector (cold item problem).
    Consequently, CF cannot compute neighborhood similarities and yields an undefined prediction.
  * Why Popularity Baseline Fails: Popularity calculates Bayesian average based on historical ratings.
    With v=0 ratings, it falls back strictly to the global mean (C=3.0) and cannot differentiate or rank the new item.
  * Why Content-Based NLP Succeeds: Content-based vectorization operates purely in the semantic text space.
    It computes TF-IDF representations directly from the headline, summary, and tags, enabling immediate
    cosine similarity matching against both existing articles and accumulated reader taste profiles.
